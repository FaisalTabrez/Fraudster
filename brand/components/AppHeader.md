# AppHeader

The logo mark + wordmark on desktop; a back button, step count and progress bar on mobile.

- Mark: `fraudster-mark.svg` (Logos), 40px on desktop, 34px on mobile; 56px for hero use. It replaces the template's typographic "F" tile.
- Wordmark "Fraudster" `wordmark` (19/700, -0.01em). Nav links 15/600 in `brand`.
- Mobile: 44px back button, "Step N of 3" in `caption`, a 4px `fr-progress` bar (`line` track, `brand` fill, `radius-4`) inset 20px.
